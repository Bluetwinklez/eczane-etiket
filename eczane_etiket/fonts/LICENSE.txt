DejaVu Sans (Regular, Bold)

DejaVu fonts are based on Bitstream Vera fonts and are released under a
permissive, redistributable license (Bitstream Vera License + DejaVu
changes, public domain-like terms). They are bundled here to guarantee
correct rendering of Turkish characters (ı, İ, ş, Ş, ğ, Ğ, ç, ö, ü) on
printed labels regardless of what fonts are installed on the end user's
computer. Full license text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
